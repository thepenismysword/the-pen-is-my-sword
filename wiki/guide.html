01 / Walkthrough

The full route spans ten runs : Normal, followed by New Game+1 through New Game+9. Earn Score to open boss doors; defeat a zone boss to advance west. Each run culminates in Heaven. The guide estimates roughly 23–26 hours to the Secret Ending for the full route.

After a completed run, progression rewinds and purchased Perks carry forward. Later cycles multiply challenge and rewards. The guide mentions Automatic Blitz + Iconic Pinball from NG+1, but does not define “Iconic Pinball” separately.

Menus, saves, and Game Over

THE HUNT is a tab within the Lexicon. CONSUME!, ADS, CREDITS, SPECIAL CODE, and REPORT A BUG are found on the COMMUNITY & CODES page.

If loading a save fails, the game displays a “couldn’t load your save” message and offers RETRY.

Each Game Over displays one ad per death, cycling in this order: Da HooliganZ → Gamer Quotient → Killer 13 ebooks → Ave Violentia → CONSUME!.

03 / Core mechanics

Attack: Tap to attack. The supplied guide gives damage per tap as 10 × 1.10^(kills) × (NG cycle + 1).

Score: Kills award Score, opening level steps and boss gates. Normal boss gates use the updated thresholds below (60% of their former values). NG+ gates continue increasing each cycle, but more slowly than before.

Blutrausch Energie: Kills generate Energie for Skills. Blood Frenzy: The combo mechanic increases attack speed; the source describes ×2.0 attack speed at +200 BLOOD FRENZY with a cap of 50, wording to confirm against the build.

Blood Debt: Killing civilians accumulates a debt that can be exchanged for power through a perk. MAXIMUM RAMPAGE: Every 1,000 manual hits grants a free Battle Bulk window.

Boss Rush: Every 90 kills in a zone, a defeated boss returns for more Score, up to two echoes per zone.

Secret Skill Fusions: Certain simultaneously active skills unlock hidden passive combinations. Erzgott’s Last Stand: Starting at NG+7, the source describes an extended True God confrontation.

ECHO'S MERCY (difficulty note)

Lose twice in a row to the same fight, whether a zone overlord, a Boss Rush echo, or a Hunt ambusher, and the opponent begins to tire. Each defeat reduces his health by 15%, down to a minimum of 50% of its original value, and reduces his damage by 10%, down to a minimum of 65%.

Your first attempt against any boss is always at full strength. ECHO'S MERCY resets when you beat the boss or start a new run. It also resets if you close the game mid-fight. TRUE CYBURN is exempt: the final duel must be faced at full strength.

06 / Secrets & codes

The guide lists six off-ladder hidden bosses, with fight order randomized per save: VICE · JUPITER · MIDBOSS · CYBURN · TRUE GOD · TRUE CYBURN. First wins award Score.

Collectibles include seven unlockable COLLECTION art plates, six rare-drop plates (listed as 15% per kill, pity at five), and seven hidden bestiary cards.

Publicly shareable special codes

XIII · XBELRX · SAFEGUARD

Enter these under Menu → COMMUNITY & CODES → SPECIAL CODE.

  • XIII: Grants the Lucky 13 perk permanently.
  • XBELRX: “He remembers you.” 13 is dropped directly in front of Cyburn.
  • SAFEGUARD: Gives 13 a shield worth half his HP, refilled only at the end of a zone.

08 / Perks

Perk Points are awarded once per 6M Score (previously every 10M).

Spend Perk Points to reshape combat and progression. The guide describes “Perk Mania” as awarding one free copy of each one-shot perk per new NG+ cycle; Armor and Mana Monster return every cycle while others lock after one purchase.

09 / Endings

The section below reveals the game's ending structure. It stays collapsed until deliberately opened.

⚠ Reveal major story and secret-ending spoilers

Normal run

Defeat the ten bosses and GOD in Heaven, followed by the Coffin Sequence, Rewind Ceremony, and NG+1.

NG+1 through NG+9

Defeat GOD in each cycle. The guide describes additional summit scenes with Erzgott, the Daemonkaiser, and an expanded NG+8 sequence.

Secret ending

On the tenth run, the golden stairs lead to the Void rather than Heaven. The source describes a tenfold Overpowered Mode, a duel against TRUE CYBURN, the Celestial Bodies joining 13, and a final sequence involving the Door and Astalon. Losing traps that save in an endless NG+ loop.
