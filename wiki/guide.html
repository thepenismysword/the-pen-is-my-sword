01 / Walkthrough

The main journey spans ten zones and continues into New Game+ cycles. Earn Score to unlock boss encounters, overcome each zone’s challenges, and carry purchased Perks into subsequent runs. Some players suspect the journey holds more than its apparent conclusion.

After a completed run, progression rewinds while purchased Perks carry forward. Later cycles increase the challenge and rewards. The guide also references Automatic Blitz + Iconic Pinball from NG+1; the precise meaning of “Iconic Pinball” remains to be confirmed.

Menus, saves, and Game Over

THE HUNT is a tab within the Lexicon. CONSUME!, ADS, CREDITS, SPECIAL CODE, and REPORT A BUG are found on the COMMUNITY & CODES page.

If loading a save fails, the game displays a “couldn’t load your save” message and offers RETRY.

Each Game Over displays one ad per death, cycling in this order: Da HooliganZ → Gamer Quotient → Killer 13 ebooks → Ave Violentia → CONSUME!.

03 / Core mechanics

Attack: Tap the arena to swing 13’s cleaver. Attack speed limits the number of swings registered per second, so improving your build matters more than tapping beyond that cap.

Score: Kills award Score, opening level steps and boss gates. Normal boss gates use the updated thresholds below (60% of their former values). NG+ gates continue increasing each cycle, but more slowly than before.

Blutrausch Energie: Kills generate Energie for Skills. Blood Frenzy: The combo mechanic increases attack speed; the source describes ×2.0 attack speed at +200 BLOOD FRENZY with a cap of 50, wording to confirm against the build.

Blood Debt: Killing civilians accumulates a debt that can be exchanged for power through a perk. MAXIMUM RAMPAGE: Every 1,000 manual hits grants a free Battle Bulk window.

Boss Rush: Every 90 kills in a zone, a defeated boss returns for more Score, up to two echoes per zone.

Secret Skill Fusions: Certain simultaneously active skills unlock hidden passive combinations. Some later encounters also introduce unexpected variations on familiar combat rules.

ECHO'S MERCY (difficulty note)

This mechanic helps players recover from difficult repeat encounters without removing the challenge of the first fight. Some major story encounters may follow different rules.

Your first attempt against a boss is always at full strength. ECHO’S MERCY resets when you beat the boss, begin a new run, or close the game mid-fight. Certain exceptional encounters may not follow these rules.

Marathon mode

Marathon is an endless-wave challenge for builds that want to push beyond the standard ladder. In v414, defeating a Marathon boss fully restores 13’s HP, accompanied by THE BLOOD RETURNS. Marathon bosses receive a reduced New Game+ damage bonus, attack more slowly, and their individual hits are capped at 10% of your HP bar. Your depth depends heavily on your build.

Celestial Body allies

Celestial Body summons can fight alongside 13, each contributing a different battlefield effect. SUMMON DESERT SANDS calls on 13’s own Celestial Body. Additional bodies and their abilities are discovered through the later story. Their identities and origins are best experienced in-game.

Art cards

Rare art cards can drop from ladder bosses at a 15% chance, with a pity guarantee by the fifth eligible attempt. Collect them in the in-game gallery.

06 / Secrets & codes

Unusual encounters can appear outside the standard zone ladder. Some are triggered by actions that are easy to overlook, and not every mystery is explained by the main route. Hidden encounter identities and conditions are intentionally omitted.

Collectibles include seven unlockable COLLECTION art plates, six rare-drop plates (listed as 15% per kill, pity at five), and seven hidden bestiary cards.

Publicly shareable special codes

XIII · XBELRX · SAFEGUARD

Enter these under Menu → COMMUNITY & CODES → SPECIAL CODE.

  • XIII: Grants the Lucky 13 perk permanently.
  • XBELRX: “He remembers you.” Triggers a special encounter. Discover its meaning in-game.
  • SAFEGUARD: Grants a reusable shield bar worth half of 13’s HP.

09 / Endings

Complete the ten-zone ladder to experience the main conclusion and the path into New Game+. As the cycles continue, pay attention to changes in the world, unusual encounters, and clues within the game itself. The wiki will leave any further discoveries to you.

Finishing a run is not necessarily the same as seeing everything the game has to offer. This section gives only broad progression guidance, without confirming or revealing any hidden conclusions.
